  WHAT THE TOOL CAN DO
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

   [1]  EDIT PDF          Type Dhivehi / English on any PDF, add your
                          signature, add stamps (or your own seal picture)
   [2]  FILL A FORM       Fill in PDF forms, including in Dhivehi
   [3]  NEW FROM TEMPLATE Make a new letter from a ready-made template
   [4]  COMPRESS          Make big PDFs smaller for email / upload

   Your files never leave your computer. Nothing is sent to the internet.


════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  GOOD TO KNOW
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

   >>  KEEP THE FOLDER TOGETHER.
       Do not move DhivehiPDF.exe out of its folder. It needs the other
       files beside it. To make a shortcut: right-click DhivehiPDF.exe
       -> Send to -> Desktop (create shortcut).

   >>  WINDOWS SAYS "Windows protected your PC"?
       This is normal for a new program. Click "More info", then
       "Run anyway". If it is blocked completely, ask IT to allow
       the DhivehiPDF folder.

   >>  SAVE YOUR RESULT.
       After building a PDF, click the Download / Save button and choose
       where to save it. Your original PDF is never changed.

   >>  YOUR SIGNATURE AND STAMP PICTURES are remembered on this computer
       only. On another computer you add them again.

   >>  YOU CAN EDIT (no need to rebuild anything):
         pdf_templates\   your letter templates
         stamps.json      the list of stamp names and colours
         fonts\           add extra Dhivehi fonts (or use the
                          "Add font..." button inside the tool)
